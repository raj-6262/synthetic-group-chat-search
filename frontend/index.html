HOW IT WORKS

Search for the idea, not the exact words.

Traditional keyword search looks for matching text. ChatSense uses semantic representations to understand what a message means.

01
⌕

Ask naturally

Type the question the way you would ask another person. You don't need to remember the exact wording.

02
◈

Understand meaning

Semantic embeddings compare the meaning of your question with the meaning of messages in the chat.

03
✓

Find the conversation

Relevant messages are returned together with sender, date, score and surrounding conversation.
